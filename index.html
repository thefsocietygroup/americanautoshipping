What Happens From Pickup to Drop Off?

(And What You Should Not Forget to Do)

What Happens at the Start?

1) Before pickup, you will hear from the auto transport carrier assigned to your vehicle. They will work with you to schedule final times to arrive at your door (or as close to it as they can get).

2) Once the car shipping company arrives and before the car is loaded, you will want to do a full inspection of your vehicle. This step is crucial — your transport truck driver will mark existing damage on a form or in an app. We highly recommend pictures are taken of all 4 sides, the top, interior, and the odometer before loading.

3) Your vehicle will now be loaded and on the way to your destination.

What Happens in Between?

Auto transport carriers travel around 400–500 miles per day and both pick up and deliver vehicles on appointment. They never just show up. Carriers will not call to schedule until they've finished the previous stop. You'll know specifically when they will arrive and where you need to meet them, hours in advance. You'll have the carrier's direct contact information including the driver's name, the dispatcher's info, and both their direct telephone numbers.

What Happens at Delivery?

1) Most carriers will call a day ahead to narrow down the delivery window and make arrangements for time and location as close to your house as they can physically get.

2) Once unloaded, do the delivery inspection. Go around the whole vehicle again — pictures on all 4 sides and the top, check the interior, and verify the odometer. Note any damages on the BOL. This must be done or you CANNOT file a claim with the carrier's insurance.

3) Congratulations, your car shipping company has delivered your vehicle! You can drive it away.

How Much Does It Cost to Ship My Car?

Pricing depends on a few key factors. Get more info on the cheapest way to ship a car.

Size & Type of Vehicle

Larger vehicles (trucks, vans) are heavier and can cost more for transport. We have plenty of experience with oversized vehicles — contact us to learn more.

Open vs Enclosed

Open transport is cheaper and more widely available. Enclosed transport costs more but provides premium protection for high-value vehicles.

Transport Distance

The further the vehicle has to go, the more expensive it will be. Cross country transport is the most expensive. To save on distance, consider taking delivery in a nearby big city.

Market Conditions & Season

Prices fluctuate based on time of year and carrier demand. Summer and snowbird season (Oct–Mar) are peak times. Booking during off-peak periods or being flexible on dates can save you money.
